Chapter 3 · The Portuguese grape varieties

4. Official Lists of Grape Varieties in Portugal

There exist three different types of documentation for

grape varieties in Portugal:

1) The list of varieties, authorized for vinification, with indication of official synonyms approved by the IVV –

Instituto da Vinha e do Vinho (Institute of Vine and Wine), which must be confirmed by the respective regional CVR (Regional Vitivinicultural Commission) for wines with an indication of origin. This list also includes the foreign varieties authorized for vinification.

2) The National Catalogue of Vine Varieties (CNV) administered by the DGAV – Diretor Geral de Alimentação

e Veterinária of the grape varieties subject to conservation breeding in Portugal and authorized for propagation. It includes 269 vine varieties, of which262 of them for wine production. EU base: Directive 68/193/CEE; Directive 2002/11/ CE, Directive 2004/29/CE, Regulation 1308/2013/CE.

3) The Coleção Ampelográfica Nacional – CAN

(National Ampelographic Vine Collection) of INIAV in Dois Portos. The CAN contains 678 wine grape and 26 table grape varieties, with detailed description of the varieties using international descriptors drawn up by the International Organization of Vine and Wine (OIV) and the molecular profiles of the 341 grape varieties authorized in Portugal for wine production. Corresponds to: Despacho n.º 3398/2012, DR n.º 48/2012, Série II de 2012-03-07, da DGAV and Portaria (Ministerial Order) n.º 380/2012, DR n.º 226/2012, Série I de 2012-11-22.
